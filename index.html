Controls

Click the game to capture the mouse. Esc lets it go; wait a moment before clicking back in.

Anywhere
WASD move, mouse looks
Shift sprint · Space jump · R respawn
At a pole
Hold W into it to grab and climb
W/S up and down · A/D circle it
Space kick off toward the camera
On top of a pole
Mouse turns · Space leap · S climb down
W take the wire the camera points along
On a wire
W/S travel · Space drop off
Hold C or X, release to slingshot · Q locks the aim
In the air
WASD steer gently · wires are grabbed automatically
Test shortcuts
1 teleport to the test spot · Z go limp
